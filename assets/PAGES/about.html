Our History

A Journey Built on Experience

From the first project to the latest, our work is defined by professional execution and a commitment to construction quality.

2017

Brindavan Infra Projects

Established 2017

The company was founded in 2017 under the name Brindavan Infra Projects, with a focus on civil engineering and construction services. This foundation laid the groundwork for the experience and expertise that defines the business today.

Now

SSB — Shree Skhandha Builders

Official New Identity

The company now officially operates as SSB — Shree Skhandha Builders. The experience gained through years of civil engineering and construction projects continues to guide every project undertaken today — with a strengthened focus on quality, professionalism and client satisfaction.
